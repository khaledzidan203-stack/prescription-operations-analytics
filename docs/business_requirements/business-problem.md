# Business Problem and Analytical Objectives

## Context

A fictional distributed service network receives operational records through several synthetic workflow categories. Local teams process records, resolve exceptions, manage scheduled work, and occasionally transfer records between sites. Management needs consistent analytics without weakening site-level data boundaries.

This scenario is intentionally generic and does not represent a real company, employer, healthcare organization, or production workflow.

## Problems with Fragmented Manual Tracking

When records are distributed across spreadsheets, messages, and local notes, organizations can experience:

- duplicate active records;
- inconsistent status definitions;
- missed scheduled work;
- incorrect site ownership assumptions;
- unresolved requirements that are difficult to aggregate;
- weak historical traceability;
- incomplete transfer lineage;
- management reports that do not reconcile with operational records;
- historical values that change when current reference values are updated.

## Public Portfolio Capabilities

The fictional platform demonstrates:

1. Synthetic record intake and validation.
2. Workflow Alpha lifecycle analysis.
3. Workflow Beta configurable decision analysis.
4. Workflow Gamma completion analysis.
5. Historical numeric snapshots.
6. Exception and resource-requirement analytics.
7. Generic fulfilment analytics.
8. Generic transfer and lineage modeling.
9. Notifications and collaboration concepts.
10. Site-level and network-level analytics with consistent KPI definitions.

## Stakeholders

| Actor | Primary need |
|---|---|
| Site user | Access and process only authorized site records |
| Network analyst | Review aggregated synthetic operational performance |
| Operations management | Reliable workload, completion, exception, and trend KPIs |
| IT/security | Controlled identity, integrity, auditability, and safe publication |

## Success Criteria

- Clear fact-table grain and stable identifiers.
- Site ownership enforced in backend examples.
- Historical snapshots remain stable.
- Duplicate scenarios are detectable.
- Dashboard totals reconcile with their supporting records.
- Unknown values remain distinct from true zero.
- Thresholds and scheduling windows are configurable fictional parameters.
- Public assets use only synthetic data and generic terminology.

## Scope Boundary

This repository is a fictionalized portfolio case study. It must not include or reconstruct real employer terminology, operational rules, internal process sequences, company data, production configuration, infrastructure, or proprietary workflow details.
